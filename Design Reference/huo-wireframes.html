  • Reputation block shows completed projects + response rate — explicitly no follower or popularity counts, per the bible's reputation principle.
  • ▲ Portfolio/Saved toggle now lives on Creative and Business profiles too, not just Consumer's — same reasoning: everyone gets a place to revisit what they've bookmarked.

Notes for next pass

  • This kit covers structure and flow only — no color, type, or brand styling. Treat it as the skeleton to hand a designer or developer, not a final look.
  • Two open items from the bible are wired as best guesses, flagged in pink above: whether Business accounts are themselves hireable/searchable, and whether Consumer's bottom-nav taps get a hard upgrade wall.
  • Not yet wired: payments/contracts, verified-work badges, and the Cleveland/Cincinnati city switcher — all listed as future features in the bible, not MVP.
