## Concept

**Huo** is the creative network connecting Ohio’s creative community — helping creatives
showcase work, discover opportunities, collaborate, and connect with businesses.

**Problem:** Columbus’s creative economy is growing, but discovery is fragmented across
Instagram, Facebook groups, and word-of-mouth.

**Solution:** A local ecosystem for portfolios, gigs, collaboration, and direct connection
to people hiring creative talent.

**Vision:** Leading creative network in Ohio → city-based model nationwide.

**Personality:** Creative, Professional, Welcoming, Innovative, Community-driven — **not**
corporate, exclusive, influencer-focused, or popularity-driven.

**Principles:** Reputation over popularity · Features must create meaningful connections ·
Local relationships matter.

## Marketplace & MVP

**Must have:** Accounts · Profiles · Feed (post / like / comment / save) · Marketplace
(post / browse / apply / review) · Messaging + notifications

**Later:** Payments, contracts, AI matching, events, groups, reviews, verified experience,
crew building, analytics

**Reputation:** Completed projects, recommendations, response rate, portfolio quality,
verified work — **avoid** public follower counts and popularity rankings.

## Monetization (Product Bible)

| Tier | Price | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Beta 1 | Free | 50 invited Columbus users |
| Beta 2 | Free | Referral to ~200 founding users |
| Founding Pro lock-in | $4.99/mo | First 200 users, lifetime founding price |
| Creative Free | $0 | Profile, portfolio, browse, basic messaging |
| Creative Pro | $9.99/mo or $99/yr | Visibility, analytics, pro tools |
| Business Free | $0 | Profile, discover, limited posts, basic messaging |
| Business Pro | $19.99/mo or $199.99/yr | Unlimited posts, hiring tools, analytics |
| Consumer | Free | Browse, request, contact, save |

Older Google Doc ranges ($14.99–$19.99 creatives, $49.99–$99.99 businesses) are **superseded**
by the Product Bible pricing above.
